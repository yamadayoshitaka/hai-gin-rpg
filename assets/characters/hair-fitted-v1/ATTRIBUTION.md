# Hairstyle Assets / 髪型素材

Original 3D model author: **Vincent Freelance** (Sketchfab: VincentFreelance).

1. Male source: [FREE Male Fashion Hair collection 01 lowpoly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-male-fashion-hair-collection-01-lowpoly-2735370f6f764078a18f5d80651050a2)
2. Female source: [12 Real-time woman Hairstyles collection 02](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/12-real-time-woman-hairstyles-collection-02-cdcc0d801e01485fa918738199f3ebd4)

Both are offered under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications:
- Extracted individual hair meshes from gallery collections.
- Removed reference mannequin heads.
- Converted coordinates to game metres and recentered hairstyles.
- Generated 3 levels of detail for mobile 50–1000 unit battles.
- Added game-side head-bone fitting and selectable color presets.

These models are not original works by the game's publisher. Preserve this
credit and the source links when distributing the game, including paid editions.
